Description

Pasta is a popular dish made with pasta and a flavorful sauce.

Ingredients

  • Pasta
  • Tomato sauce
  • Olive oil
  • Garlic
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Boil water in a pot.
  2. Cook the pasta according to the package instructions.
  3. Prepare the sauce with olive oil, garlic, and tomato sauce.
  4. Mix the cooked pasta with the sauce.
  5. Season and serve.
