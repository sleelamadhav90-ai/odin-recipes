PANCAKES

Home

Description

Pancakes are a delicious breakfast item made from a simple batter and cooked on a griddle or in a pan.

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Milk
  • Eggs
  • Sugar
  • Baking powder
  • Butter

Steps

  1. Mix the dry ingredients in a bowl.
  2. In another bowl, whisk the wet ingredients.
  3. Combine the wet and dry ingredients until just mixed.
  4. Heat a lightly oiled griddle or pan over medium-high heat.
  5. Pour the batter onto the griddle to form pancakes.
  6. Cook until bubbles form on the surface, then flip and cook until golden brown.
  7. Serve hot with your favorite toppings.
