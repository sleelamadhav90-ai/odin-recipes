Description

Lasagna is a delicious Italian dish made with layers of pasta, sauce, cheese, and other flavorful ingredients.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna sheets
  • Tomato sauce
  • Ground meat
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Prepare the tomato sauce and cook the meat.
  3. Spread some sauce in a baking dish.
  4. Layer the lasagna sheets, sauce, and cheese.
  5. Repeat the layers until the ingredients are used.
  6. Bake until the pasta is tender and the cheese is golden.
  7. Let it cool slightly before serving.
